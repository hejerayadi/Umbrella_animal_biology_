# Trait Discovery Agent

## Objective
Automatically identify and explain the genes, proteins, and biological pathways associated with specific animal traits, and compare those traits across species.

## Problem Addressed
Biological knowledge about traits is scattered across many databases and scientific papers. Researchers often spend considerable time searching Gene Ontology terms, exploring KEGG pathways, looking through UniProt entries, and reading PubMed articles.

## Main Tasks
- Find genes linked to specific animal traits.
- Identify proteins and pathways involved in those traits.
- Compare trait biology across species.
- Centralize evidence from ontology, pathway, protein, and literature sources.
- Provide explainable answers backed by biological data.
