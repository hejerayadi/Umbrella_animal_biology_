# Biodiversity Agent

## Objective
Analyze and visualize the spatial distribution of animal species, identify biodiversity hotspots, study habitat changes, and support conservation efforts by integrating occurrence and conservation status data.

## Problem Addressed
Species distributions change continuously because of climate change, habitat destruction, human activities, migration patterns, and extinction risks. These data are often scattered across multiple databases and are difficult to analyze manually.

## Main Tasks
- Map species distributions.
- Identify biodiversity hotspots.
- Track habitat change over time.
- Integrate occurrence and conservation data.
- Produce interactive biodiversity insights for conservation analysis.
